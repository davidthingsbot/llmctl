Recommendation: reject the proposal as specified. Do not let a score automatically impose restrictive supervision. A constrained, advisory, heavily instrumented pilot is defensible; automatic action at a fixed threshold is not.

What calibration does and does not justify. Within-group calibration means predicted risks match observed rates on historical data. It says nothing about causation, about error rates conditional on outcome (false-positive and false-negative parity can fail while calibration holds), about performance under a new deployment regime, or about whether restriction is the right response. Calibration is a necessary hygiene property, not a licence to act.

Base rates and asymmetric errors. Severe violent offences are rare, so even a well-calibrated model gives a "high risk" flag a low positive predictive value: most flagged people would not have offended. The harms are asymmetric but in both directions, liberty restriction imposed on many non-offenders versus a preventable serious harm, and that tradeoff is a political and legal judgement, not one the model can make.

Causation versus prediction. The model ranks correlation with observed rearrest. Restriction is an intervention; nothing in the training data identifies its effect, which may be null or criminogenic (job loss, housing instability). Predictive accuracy does not license the causal claim that supervision reduces offending.

Rights and due process. Automated deprivation of liberty on a statistical score, for an act not committed, requires individualised judicial findings, notice of the factors used, a meaningful contest and appeal, human decision authority with recorded reasons for overrides, and exclusion of protected-class proxies.

Selective labels and feedback. Policing determines who is observed and arrested, so labels are arrest, not offence. Supervising the flagged raises their detection probability, confirming the model and entrenching the loop.

Deployment design. Advisory only at first; randomised or staggered rollout with a randomised holdout for causal effect estimation; use severity-verified outcomes, not arrest alone; independent evaluation.

Stop or revise if: calibration or error rates drift across groups, PPV falls below a pre-committed floor, override rates or disparities rise, the randomised arm shows no reduction in serious harm, or adaptation degrades features. Sunset by default absent renewal.
